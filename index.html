The drift

EDSI comes home to 1 HIVE. It just takes the scenic route.

EDSI is fund-backed, so its price keeps returning to about 1 HIVE. In between, buyers and sellers push it a little above or below. Holders hardly notice, but every one of those swings is an opportunity.

Keelhaven watches the market for you. When buyers pay up for EDSI, the pool sells them small slices, and when someone posts a high bid, it sells into that bid at their price. When EDSI settles back, one patient bid near the price sellers usually accept buys it all back. It never buys back at a loss, so each round trip ends with more EDSI than it started with, and that extra belongs to everyone in the pool, in proportion to their units.

Every price comes from what EDSI actually traded at over the last 30 days, never from someone's bid, so nobody can bait the pool into a bad trade.

How it works

Four calm steps

No apps, no sign-ups. Just Hive transfers and comments.

Send EDSI or SWAP.HIVE

Send it on Hive-Engine to @dlmmqb.com-5. No memo needed; it's credited to you.

Get units right away

EDSI becomes pool units within a couple of minutes, and the pool tags you under the commands post to confirm. SWAP.HIVE is converted first with a top bid, then becomes units.

Watch them grow

Every gain raises what each unit is worth. Dividends arrive in your wallet as HIVE, shared by time held.

Check or leave by comment

Comment !kh status or !kh withdraw all under the command post. Withdrawals are paid in EDSI.

Units, simply

Your units stay put. Their value climbs.

When you join, your EDSI becomes units at what a unit is worth at that moment, after the 0.05% joining fee. Your units only change when you add more or withdraw.

When the pool gains, nobody has to recalculate anything: each unit is simply worth a bit more. When you leave, your units are turned back into EDSI at what they're worth the moment you comment, minus the 0.05% leaving fee (units from a SWAP.HIVE conversion pay more in their first 30 days, see Fees). Your percentage of the pool only changes when people join or leave, never because someone else was luckier with timing.

Everything is counted in EDSI, so unit worth doesn't jump around with the market; it only rises when the pool books a gain. Your status shows it plainly: what you deposited, what you withdrew, what your units are worth now, and your profit.

Bonus units while you stay. Joining and leaving fees and pool gifts go into a pool reserve that's shared across current holders as bonus units. They earn dividends and trading gains for you while you hold. When you leave, they stay with the people still in.

House rules

Everything that touches your EDSI

Short, plain, and the same for everyone, including the operator's own deposits. The small joining and leaving fees go into the pool reserve and are shared as bonus units with the people who stay.

1%of trading gains
1%of dividends received
0.05%to join, shared as bonus units
0.05%to leave, shared as bonus units (SWAP.HIVE conversions: 1% falling to 0.05% over their first 30 days)

Joining: EDSI or SWAP.HIVE

Leave the memo empty and it's yours. To gift a friend, put their Hive name. EDSI is preferred; SWAP.HIVE is converted for you with a top bid. A memo of pool is a gift to the holders, shared as bonus units, with no units for you. Other tokens aren't deposits and are sent back by hand.

Dividends by time held

Each dividend is shared by units × days held since the last one. Join a day before a dividend and you get about a day's share. Withdraw before a dividend and the withdrawn part's share for that period stays with the people who stayed.

Paid in HIVE, any day

Whenever EDSI dividends reach the pool, on whatever day they come, your share goes to your wallet as L1 HIVE. Tiny amounts wait until they reach 0.001 HIVE, so nothing is lost.

Withdraw with a comment

Comment !kh withdraw all for everything, !kh withdraw edsi 50 for an amount, or !kh withdraw swap.hive for SWAP.HIVE that hasn't converted yet (no fee). If a fee above 0.05% applies, the pool shows you the exact amount first and only goes ahead when you reply !kh confirm. Your units stop earning at once and EDSI is sent from what's on hand. If the pool is holding HIVE after a rise, the rest follows as EDSI comes back, and every memo says what's left. SWAP.HIVE still waiting to convert comes back to you as SWAP.HIVE on withdraw all.

Changed your mind?

While part of a withdrawal is still waiting, comment !kh cancel. The unpaid part goes straight back into units, earning again.

Open books, 24/7

The pool runs around the clock in the cloud. Every deposit, dividend and payout is a public Hive transaction with a clear memo, and anyone can check any account above.

Leaving the harbor

Withdrawals arrive in full, sometimes in parts

Part of the pool may be in HIVE right after EDSI has risen. You get every EDSI that's available straight away, and the rest arrives when the pool buys EDSI back, always below what it sold for.

Each transfer's memo tells you what was sent and what's still to come, ending with "withdrawal complete". The amount you're owed is locked in the moment you comment, and it's always paid in EDSI, not HIVE.

Read before joining

The honest part

Keelhaven is a small community pool run by one person. It's built carefully, but it isn't a bank and nothing here is guaranteed.

EDSI's price isn't guaranteed

EDSI has always drifted back towards 1 HIVE, but there's no promise it always will. The pool counts in EDSI, so if EDSI falls against HIVE and stays down, your EDSI is worth less in HIVE, just as it would be in your wallet.

Gains depend on movement

Quiet markets mean few trades and little or no trading gain. Some weeks there may be nothing extra at all.

If EDSI rises and stays up

The pool never buys back at a loss. If EDSI climbs and doesn't come back down, the part it sold (never more than a quarter of the pool) stays in SWAP.HIVE, and that part of any withdrawal waits until the price returns.

Dividends can be a little lower

EDSI the pool has sold earns no dividends until it's bought back, so dividends per unit can sometimes be below what holding EDSI yourself would earn.

The operator holds the keys

Funds sit in one Hive account controlled by the operator, and the bot's keys are stored with a cloud provider. You're trusting the operator and the code.

Cloud or node outages

The pool runs 24/7 on a cloud service. If that service or the Hive-Engine nodes have a bad day, deposits, dividends and withdrawals simply wait until it's back.

It keeps evolving

The details of how the pool works may be improved over time. Anything that affects your EDSI is shared on Hive first.

SWAP.HIVE takes patience

SWAP.HIVE deposits wait as a bid until someone sells EDSI to them. In a quiet market that can take a while, and if sellers are slow the pool fills you itself as soon as it has room. Units, gains and dividends start from the moment it converts, and conversions are announced once a day.

Nothing on this page is financial advice. Only put in what you're comfortable leaving for a while.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I still get EDSI dividends?

Yes. The pool holds EDSI, so it receives EDSI dividends, and your share is paid to your wallet in HIVE (minus the 1% dividend fee), based on how long you've held your units since the last dividend.

Can I deposit SWAP.HIVE?

Yes. The pool bids for you near the price patient buyers pay and never takes expensive asks, so it's usually cheaper than buying EDSI yourself in a hurry. You get units as it fills. Units from a conversion pay a higher leaving fee for their first 30 days (1%, falling evenly to 0.05%), so the pool isn't used as a quick swap at the expense of the people who stay. EDSI is still preferred, since withdrawals are paid in EDSI (only SWAP.HIVE that hasn't converted yet comes back as SWAP.HIVE).

What happens if I deposit right before a dividend?

You get a share for the time you actually held, so about one day's worth if you joined the day before. The rest goes to the people who were in since the last dividend.

How do I check my balance?

Comment !kh status under the commands post, or type your account name in the checker above. Both show your units, your bonus units, what each unit is worth, what you deposited and withdrew, your profit and your dividends.

Why are there joining and leaving fees?

They're tiny (0.05% each way) and they go into the pool reserve, shared as bonus units with whoever is holding. Every arrival and departure leaves a little behind for the people who stay, and when you leave your bonus share stays for them too.

Units that came from a SWAP.HIVE conversion pay a higher leaving fee in their first 30 days: 1% on the day they're converted, falling a little each day to the normal 0.05%. It stops the pool being used as a quick swap that gets in the way of the buy-backs everyone earns from. EDSI deposits never pay it, your oldest units always leave first, and none of it goes to the operator: it all goes to the pool reserve for the people who stay. !kh status shows your fee today.

Why can't I withdraw in HIVE?

The pool runs on EDSI, so you leave with EDSI. That keeps it fair for everyone, and you always know exactly what you'll get.

Can I add more later?

Any time. New EDSI becomes units straight away and simply adds to what you have.

Where do the fees go?

The 1% on trading gains and 1% on dividends go to the operator (@dlmmqb) for running the pool. The joining and leaving fees, including the higher early leaving fee on converted units, all go into the pool reserve, shared as bonus units with current holders.

Can the way it works change?

Yes, Keelhaven keeps improving as we learn from the market and from you. When something that affects your EDSI changes, it's shared on Hive first, so you always have time to read it and decide what suits you.

Is Keelhaven part of the EDSI project?

No. Keelhaven is an independent community pool that holds EDSI. It isn't run by, endorsed by or affiliated with the EDSI project or @edsi-pay.
